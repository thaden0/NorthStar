# Recommended Features for North Star

Based on the current architecture (AI-powered, Contractor/Agency focus) and market standards for freelancer platforms, here are the 10 best features to implement next.

## 1. AI Proposal & SOW Generator

**Problem:** Writing proposals is time-consuming and repetitive.
**Feature:** A tool where you input raw client notes or requirements, and the AI Agent generates a professional PDF proposal with:

- Scope of Work (SOW)
- Deliverables & Timeline
- Pricing packages (calculated from your rate settings)
- Legal terms (boilerplate)
  **Why for North Star:** Leverages your existing `Agent Service` and `Settings` (billable rates).

## 2. Collaborative Client Portal

**Problem:** Clients send emails asking "Where are we on this?" or "Can you resend that file?"
**Feature:** A secure, password-protected area where clients can:

- View real-time project progress (Kanban/Timeline)
- Download deliverables (Assets)
- Pay invoices
- Approve milestones
  **Why for North Star:** Elevates the "Personal Dashboard" into a professional business platform.

## 3. Intelligent Invoicing & Payments

**Problem:** Manual billing is prone to errors and often delayed.
**Feature:** An invoicing module that:

- Pulls data directly from the **Time Tracking** module.
- Auto-generates invoices on set schedules (e.g., bi-weekly).
- Integrates with Stripe/PayPal for one-click payment.
- Sends smart reminders for overdue payments.
  **Why for North Star:** You already have the settings for "Billable Rate" and "Time Tracking" – this completes the loop.

## 4. Meeting Intelligence Hub

**Problem:** Losing track of action items from client calls.
**Feature:** A system to upload meeting recordings (or connect to Zoom/Meet) where the AI:

- Transcribes the audio.
- Generates an executive summary.
- Extracts "Action Items" and auto-creates Tasks in your dashboard.
- Detects sentiment/client friction points.
  **Why for North Star:** Perfect use case for your AI Agent service capabilities.

## 5. Smart CRM & Lead Pipeline

**Problem:** Managing potential work in spreadsheets or messy contacts lists.
**Feature:** Upgrade the existing "Contacts" widget into a full CRM:

- **Kanban Board:** Leads -> Contacted -> Proposal Sent -> Negotiation -> Closed.
- **Lead Scoring:** AI analyzes email history to score how likely a client is to convert.
- **Automated Follow-ups:** Reminders to ping clients who haven't responded.
  **Why for North Star:** Essential for maintaining a steady stream of contract work.

## 6. Dynamic Project Timeline

**Problem:** Task lists don't show the "big picture" of deadlines.
**Feature:** A visual Gantt chart or timeline view that:

- Visualizes overlapping project schedules.
- Alerts you to capacity bottlenecks (e.g., "You're overbooked next week").
- Auto-adjusts based on time-tracking data (e.g., "This task is taking 20% longer than expected").
  **Why for North Star:** Critical for managing multiple contracts simultaneously.

## 7. Knowledge Base / "Second Brain"

**Problem:** Repeating the same answers to clients or forgetting technical solutions.
**Feature:** A wiki/documentation system where:

- You store code snippets, SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures), and reusable assets.
- Users (or you) can query it via AI ("How do we handle 404s in the React project?").
- Can be shared publicly (Portfolio) or privately (Client Portal).
  **Why for North Star:** Fits the "North Star" theme of guiding/documentation.

## 8. Financial Health Dashboard

**Problem:** Not knowing your true effective hourly rate or tax liability.
**Feature:** A dedicated analytics view for:

- Income vs. Expenses (with receipt scanning).
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) tracking.
- Tax estimation bucket.
- Profitability per client (Cost of labor vs. Revenue).
  **Why for North Star:** The "business" side of the dashboard that complements the "work" side.

## 9. Contract & E-Signature Module

**Problem:** paying for DocuSign/HelloSign for simple contractor agreements.
**Feature:** Built-in contract management:

- Templated agreements (NDA, MSA, Contractor Agreement).
- Digital signature fields.
- Audit trail of who signed and when.
  **Why for North Star:** Reduces friction in onboarding new clients.

## 10. Automated Onboarding Flows

**Problem:** Onboarding a new client involves 10 different emails and file requests.
**Feature:** A "Magic Link" you send to new clients that walks them through:

- Filling out their profile/billing info.
- Uploading necessary assets (logos, credentials).
- Signing the contract.
- Joining the Slack/Discord/Trello board.
  **Why for North Star:** Automates the most administrative-heavy part of starting a new gig.
